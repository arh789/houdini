# Current Free Houdini Video Learning Path

This is the corrected, current-first viewing order for the minimalist Houdini project. Everything is free; the two SideFX-hosted courses are marked.

## Foundations

1. [H22 Foundations: Welcome](https://www.sidefx.com/tutorials/h22-foundations-welcome/) — 1h 11m
   Current interface, SOP workflow, simulation, caching and rendering. SideFX-hosted.

## Packed RBD and caching

2. [Mastering Houdini Destruction Simplified — Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yzwwPs80bZw) — 1h 25m
   Packed pieces, `name`, proxy geometry, RBD simulation and transform caches.

3. [Best Way to Create and Cache Colliders](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xAalf7paYUk) — 14m
   Independent collider caches and proxy geometry.

## FLIP and RBD interaction

4. [Fluid Nodes in Houdini 19.5 — SOP FLIP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86nbP2Q7BKA) — 28m
   Bounded SOP FLIP, sources, collisions and local bounds.

5. [Houdini 20 — Combine FLIP and RBD Simulations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A3z-t4oV5l8) — 22m
   Direct RBD/FLIP integration.

6. [Houdini 20 FLIP/Whitewater SOP Course — Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L-o0SEijZ7U) — approximately 26m
   Current particle caching, fluid meshing and mesh caching. Skip the whitewater sections.

## Impact event and local POP refinement

7. [Understanding Houdini — Look Mom, I’m Coding in VEX](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QNOPje4Qt0Y) — 24m
   Vectors, parameters, distance calculations and falloff masks.

8. [Houdini to Niagara — Impact Data](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P3I8k5JqaI8) — 19m
   Extracting RBD impact position, velocity and related attributes. Ignore the Niagara export portion.

9. [Attribute Transfer, Attribute Copy and ID Attributes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=49DRSO3pPQU) — 8m
   Transferring the event-derived refinement mask.

10. [Intro to Houdini Particles — Full Beginner Course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=94YAomHfMbw)
    Watch **50:14–1:33:58** only: POP sourcing, inherited velocity, attributes and source interpolation.

## Solaris and rendering

11. [Introduction to Solaris and Karma — Houdini 20 playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLopImPTpJclS7Ug5q5lsROrNDzZsI1jVE) — 1h 9m
    USD assembly, MaterialX, lighting, camera and Karma XPU rendering.

## Best project overview

12. [H21 Foundations: Smashing Wineglass](https://www.sidefx.com/tutorials/h21-foundations-smashing-wineglass/) — 1h 7m
    Modern RBD, FLIP, caching, USD, Solaris and Karma in one project. SideFX-hosted. Use this as the overview; the other videos supply the deeper details.
